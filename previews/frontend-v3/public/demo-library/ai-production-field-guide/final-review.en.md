# Review a final cut in two passes

Export a separate preview of the demo cut of Last Signal. Keep its version number visible so both passes refer to the same file.

## Watch once as a viewer

Play from the first frame to black without pausing. Note when you first know the train is not coming. If the answer only arrives in the credits, revisit the traveler's gaze and the visual clue between ten and twenty seconds.

## Inspect the delivery details

- Check that the lowered gaze and sound change land on the same beat.
- Confirm both captions can be read and have enough space on a small screen.
- Inspect the middle light for unintended flicker and the blackout for a stray frame.
- Check resolution, aspect ratio and file naming against the export sheet.

Log a time and verification result for each fix. Watch at least the two neighboring shots again after any edit.
