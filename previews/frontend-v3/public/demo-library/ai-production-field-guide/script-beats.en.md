# Plan the beats of a thirty-second short

Last Signal is a fictional film about a traveler waiting on an almost empty platform. A distant light seems to be the last train, until a departure sign lights up. The audience needs to understand one shift in expectation.

## Give each phase a job

| Time | What the audience sees | What they infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00–10 s | Station sign, empty tracks, distant light | A train is about to enter |
| 10–20 s | The light steadies and the traveler looks down | The light may be on the platform |
| 20–30 s | The departure sign illuminates and the wide shot falls quiet | The service has ended, leaving a final goodbye |

## Read it as still frames first

Lay out three panels and read them left to right. Leave the explanatory voice-over aside. Ask a colleague where the expectation changed. If the answer is unclear, reorder the information in the middle panel before adding motion.
