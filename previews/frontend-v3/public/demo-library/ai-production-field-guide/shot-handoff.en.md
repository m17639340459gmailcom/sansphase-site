# Hand off a storyboard

Each shot in Last Signal gets one visible duty. “Cinematic and realistic” can establish tone, but does not tell the next editor when to revise a frame.

## Specify framing, motion and the out point

- S01, platform wide. Sign on the left, track perspective on the right. Hold for two seconds after the light appears.
- S02, traveler close-up. The traveler raises their gaze, then looks toward the sign. Leave empty space on the right to establish the direction.
- S03, locked-off sign. Hold after the text turns on so it can be read, with two seconds reserved for a caption.

## Check continuity before handing it off

Give the sound editor a lightweight preview with timecode. Keep the cool light coming from the same direction across all three shots. Flag reversed direction, unexplained flicker or illegible signs before the handoff.
