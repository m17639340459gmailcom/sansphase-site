# Record a lesson as a repeatable experiment

Window Light is a fictional tabletop photography exercise. At four in the afternoon, the rim of the cup should stay legible while the view beyond the window recedes. Test that question with three frames before planning the final shoot.

## Change one condition at a time

Fix the camera at the edge of the desk and keep the shutter speed, aperture and focal length fixed. Position the same white card five, fifteen and thirty centimeters to the right of the cup. Label the frames W01, W02 and W03.

| Frame | Adjustment | Visible difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| W01 | Card at five centimeters | Bright rim, flattened desk texture |
| W02 | Card at fifteen centimeters | Clear silhouette and a softer reflection |
| W03 | Card at thirty centimeters | The right side of the rim blends into the background |

## Leave enough information to start again

Reproduce W02 first, then repeat the experiment on an overcast day. Keep the new set separate if the direction of the light changes. Save the three raw frames, an overhead photo of the desk and this note, so the exercise can resume a week later.
